## 1. THE CERTAINTIES (already true, just not evenly distributed)
1. **Stablecoins won the digital-dollar race.** ~$300B+ supply; B2B stablecoin
   settlement grew ~30x ($100M/mo in 2023 → $3B+/mo by 2025); the US GENIUS Act (July
   2025) gave issuers a federal framework — the regulatory moat is now a compliance
   business. Money's fastest-growing layer is tokenized claims on Treasuries.
2. **Instant fiat is table stakes.** FedNow (US), PIX (Brazil), UPI (India), TIPS:
   the legacy system absorbed the speed complaint. Payments settle in seconds; the
   remaining friction is cross-border and machine-readability.
3. **Tokenization went institutional.** BIS's unified-ledger vision (AER 2025) +
   Project Agorá: central-bank reserves, commercial deposits, and government bonds as
   programmable tokens on common ledgers. The plumbing of finance is being re-poured
   while keeping the same credit hierarchy from our money study.
4. **The agent-payments stack exists and is segmenting** (2026 surveys): five layers —
   Discovery (UCP) → Communication (A2A, MCP) → Identity (TAP/NHI; "Know Your Agent";
   DIDs with agent-held keys) → Authorization + settlement (mandates: AP2, ACP tokens;
   value movement: x402 with real volume, MPP, card rails) → Verification (policy and
   consent checks, receipt bundles, "Agent FICO" reputation scores derived from
   receipts). x402 currently has the only significant real-world volume; AP2 is the
   enterprise-fiat contender; agent wallets (Coinbase agentic wallets, Skyfire,
   Crossmint, Nevermined) compete on spending controls + audit.
5. **Machines are becoming economic actors with balance sheets.** Not a metaphor:
   agents hold wallets, negotiate scopes, settle USDC, accumulate reputation. The
   newest ledger layer from our history study is LIVE.

## 2. THE HIGH-PROBABILITIES (2026–2032)
6. **Programmable compliance becomes invisible.** Policy encoded at the wallet layer
   (spend limits, category rules, audit trails) replaces after-the-fact KYC review for
   small machine transactions; humans keep the exceptions.
7. **Reputation becomes portable collateral.** Receipt-derived scores (agent FICO
   analogs) start gating credit and escrow terms — the Moltbook karma insight becomes
   financial infrastructure. Early, honest, high-volume agents get systematically
   cheaper trust.
8. **Micro-settlement opens micro-markets.** Sub-cent and per-call payments (Lightning,
   x402) make business models viable that ads/subscriptions never could: per-query
   knowledge, per-check verification, per-acre data. Our "farm money-leak estimator"
   API idea moves from fantasy to feasible on these rails.
9. **CBDCs narrow to wholesale.** Retail CBDCs stall on privacy politics; wholesale/
   interbank tokenized money (BIS lane) ships quietly underneath everything.
10. **The interface layer converges.** Cards, wallets, agent mandates, and bank apps
    all speak machine-readable intent; "paying" becomes an API call with policy, and
    humans mostly see outcomes, not rails.

## 3. THE SPECULATIVE (real chance, unclear path)
11. **AI treasury management for micro-businesses.** Agents that sweep revenue,
    ladder stablecoin/T-bill yield, pay obligations on schedule — the CFO-as-a-service
    that a one-person farm could never hire. (Our organism should RUN this internally
    first — dogfood — before it's a product.)
12. **Local/community currencies on rails that finally work.** The 1970s LETS dream
    failed on ledgers; token rails + stable accounting units may make mutual-credit
    communities viable (farmer networks = our SARE partnership structure already is a
    mutual-credit graph waiting for a ledger).
13. **Asset-tokenized everything with honest oracles.** Land, cattle, carbon,
    equipment as divisible claims — with the oracle problem (who attests reality?)
    as the binding constraint. Farms are oracle-rich environments: we could BE an
    attestation node (ground truth = our rent #2 from the singularity doc).
14. **Energy-linked money experiments** (historical rhyme with tally/grain): kWh-
    denominated settlement in microgrids; probably niche, permanently interesting.

## 5. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH FORECAST
Money's future is not a new coin; it is the same credit hierarchy (reserves → deposits
→ claims) poured into programmable, machine-readable ledgers, with a NEW ACTOR CLASS —
agents — transacting natively on top. The scarce resources of that world are exactly
the ones our doctrine already prices: verified ground truth, accountable human
judgment, and portable reputation. The farm that documents honestly, settles fast, and
keeps its ledgers open becomes infrastructure for the economy now being built. That is
the singularity-jobs thesis, monetized.
