## 1. THE FIVE MECHANISMS (what actually changes)
1. **Zero marginal cost of cognition.** Digital labor/capital reproduce near-free
   (Brynjolfsson & Benzell, "Digital Abundance and Scarce Genius," Stanford Digital
   Economy Lab 2025; Catalini et al., "Some Simple Economics of AGI," arXiv 2602.20946).
   Altman: "intelligence too cheap to meter" (Fortune 2026-03-12). Anything capturable
   by a metric gets absorbed — including creative/analytical work.
2. **The verification bottleneck.** Catalini's core model: two racing cost curves —
   exponential Cost-to-Automate vs biologically bottlenecked Cost-to-Verify. The binding
   constraint on growth becomes human VERIFICATION BANDWIDTH, not intelligence. A
   measurability gap opens between what agents can execute and what humans can afford
   to check. Technical change becomes MEASURABILITY-BIASED: value migrates to what is
   hard to measure.
3. **Genius/superstar scarcity.** With labor and capital elastic, the inelastic third
   factor — judgment, taste, responsibility-bearing "genius" — absorbs the rents
   (Brynjolfsson & Benzell). Median wages stagnate while superstar returns grow; growth
   is constrained by the scarce input, which for a one-person firm is FOUNDER HOURS.
4. **Provenance premium.** When synthetic content floods, VERIFIED-human,
   provenance-marked content appreciates (Catalini: "rents migrate to verification-grade
   ground truth and cryptographic provenance"). Trust shifts from "who made this" to
   "who underwrote this."
5. **Unstable middle.** Catalini's failure modes: Missing Junior Loop (apprenticeship
   collapses — no more cheap on-ramp skills), Codifier's Curse (experts who codify
   their judgment into AI erode their own moat), Trojan-Horse externality (unverified
   AI output deployed anyway), Hollow Economy vs Augmented Economy (the fork: scale
   verification WITH capability → unbounded experimentation).

## 2. THE FIVE RENTS (where value accrues in an abundant-intelligence world)
R1. **Underwriting / liability-bearing** — insuring outcomes, not generating them.
R2. **Ground truth** — real-world datasets only a physical operation produces.
R3. **Provenance** — verifiable human/authentic origin.
R4. **Physical presence** — bodies, places, hands (never digitizable).
R5. **Relationships & reputation** — trust graphs that survive verification.
(These five are the micro-scale mirror of the macro papers above.)
