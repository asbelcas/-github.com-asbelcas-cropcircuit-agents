## 1. WHAT MONEY IS
Three functions (St. Louis Fed): medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value.
Deeper: money is a social relation — an IOU everyone trusts. A dollar is not a thing; it
is a claim on the production of everyone else who accepts dollars. "Money is memory"
(Kocherlakota): it records who did what for whom so we don't need barter coincidence.
When we say something is "worth $350," we mean: strangers will surrender that many
generic claims-on-everything in exchange for it. Price is the crowd's ranking of value
against the claim-unit.

## 2. WHERE MONEY COMES FROM (the layers, bottom to top)
- LAYER 0 — State issuance. Government spends currency into existence and taxes some
  back (chartal anchor; seigniorage on coins; central-bank reserves as bankers' money).
  Net government spending = new money at the root.
- LAYER 1 — Bank credit. THE FACT THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING (BoE 2014): commercial banks
  do NOT lend out deposits. When a bank makes a loan it CREATES a matching deposit —
  new money, typed into a ledger. ~97% of all money is bank deposits created this way.
  Repayment destroys money; interest requires the economy to keep producing new value to
  service the stock of claims. Money is born as a promise to produce future value.
- LAYER 2 — Payment processors & tokens. PayPal/ACH = ledgers re-marking claims on L1
  deposits (often no bank dollar moves at all). Stablecoins = tokenized claims on
  Treasury/bank assets. BTC = scarcity-anchored commodity-money narrative (no issuer).
- SETTLEMENT HIERARCHY: central-bank reserves settle banks; deposits settle people;
  processor balances settle app users; tokens settle chain users. Our whole wallet-math
  problem lives in the top two rungs of this ladder.

## 3. THE FIVE DOORS (every way money can enter a wallet)
1. EXCHANGE — sell something someone values above the money. (Value out, money in.)
2. CLAIM — wages, rent, interest, refunds, unclaimed property: a pre-existing legal or
   social claim on others' production, collected.
3. GIFT — donations, grants, tips: others' production transferred without exchange.
   **This is "asking." PARKED per Casey's directive 2026-09-17.**
4. REVALUATION — assets you already hold appreciate. Requires capital first. (N/A at $0.)
5. ISSUANCE — create money/credit against your own future production: a loan, an
   invoice, a gift card, a community currency. The business version: PRESELL. Take
   payment now against contracted future delivery and you have issued a small,
   honest, fully-backed-by-production money. (Sprint intake → payment → delivery is
   exactly this, one customer at a time.)
