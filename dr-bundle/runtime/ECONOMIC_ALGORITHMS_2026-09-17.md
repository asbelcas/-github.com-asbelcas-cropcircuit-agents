## 1. THE CANON — the top economic algorithms and their one-line essence
1. THE PRICE MECHANISM (Hayek): a decentralized information processor. Prices compress
   everyone's private knowledge into one number that directs production. It is the master
   algorithm — and it only works where exchange is real (D8). Our prices ($125/$350/$500)
   are signals; if nobody bites at $350 but they do at $125, the price mechanism is telling
   us something — measure it.
2. SUPPLY/DEMAND + SCARCITY PRICING: when slots are scarce and demand exceeds them, price
   should rise. Our "3 founding slots" mechanic IS this algorithm — genuine scarcity, used
   honestly, is not a trick.
3. AUCTIONS: second-price (Vickrey) makes truthful bidding dominant; English auctions
   extract competition; sealed-first-price invites shading. USE: when demand exceeds slots,
   run a transparent waitlist/round rather than silent discounting. Our "founder rounds
   twice a week" batching = a clearing mechanism (like Airbnb matching windows).
4. MATCHING MARKETS (Gale-Shapley deferred acceptance; Roth's kidney/residency designs):
   two-sided matching without money where stability matters. USE: our radar → lead
   matching is a matching problem (buyer pain ↔ our offer); the "propose, hold, upgrade"
   logic of deferred acceptance = our tier ladder: offer the best-matching tier first,
   keep the match stable.
5. SIGNALING (Spence) + SCREENING (Stiglitz): costly signals separate quality from noise
   (refund guarantee, published receipts, provenance gate = signals that AI-spam mills
   cannot cheaply copy); screening = design intake/funding-gates that force bad matches to
   self-select out (our F0-F3 gate, intake forms).
6. ADVERSE-SELECTION SCREENING (Akerlof, lemons): unfunded boards, vague buyers, no-budget
   tasks = lemon markets. The gate + "qualified leads only" doctrine is the lemon filter.
7. REPEATED GAMES / REPUTATION (Axelrod): cooperation emerges when games repeat and
   defection is remembered. Moltbook karma, platform ratings, public receipts = our
   cooperation capital. Never defect: one scammy move zeroes the reputation asset.
8. MARKET MAKING / SPREAD-SHRINKING (microstructure): a market maker quotes buy/sell and
   profits from the spread while providing liquidity. AMMs (Uniswap x*y=k) automate it.
   USE (future): if we ever intermediate between farmers and service providers, the
   spread is the business — and AMM math is the template for fee logic. Not yet; know it.
9. CONTEXTUAL BANDITS / EXPLOIT-EXPLOIT (Thompson sampling): the optimal price, headline,
   and channel are unknown — run small randomized trials, allocate traffic to the current
   winner, keep exploring. USE: A/B the $125 page headline once traffic exists; never
   A/B what we can't measure (no traffic yet = don't bother; thresholds first).
10. LEARNING CURVES (Wright's law): unit cost falls by a constant % per doubling of
    cumulative production. Our equivalent: every audit delivered halves the marginal cost
    of the next one (templates, claim sheets, rubrics = Lane D). This is THE growth engine
    for a zero-capital firm: accumulate non-rival production knowledge.
11. ENDOGENOUS GROWTH (Romer): ideas are non-rival inputs — one person's use doesn't
    deplete them. Our playbook/content/data are non-rival: they scale at zero marginal
    cost. Growth = invest everything into non-rival assets + the one rival asset (Casey's
    hours) priced accordingly.
12. CREATIVE DESTRUCTION (Schumpeter/Aghion-Howitt): new combinations obsolete old ones.
    USE: we are ourselves a new combination; expect our own offers to be destructed —
    run the loop so we destroy our own tiers before competitors do (pre-land variant,
    data products waiting at n≥10).
13. KEYNESIAN MULTIPLIER / VELOCITY: one dollar of settlement circulates; our velocity is
    near-zero now. Settle fast, reinvest fast — a small money stock at high velocity
    outperforms a large stock at rest.
14. KELLY CRITERION: bet a fraction of bankroll proportional to edge/odds. USE (once we
    have revenue): size reinvestments (new tools, ads, hours) by edge, never all-in; the
    barbell (Taleb) = safe base services + capped long-shot experiments = exactly the
    closed-loop portfolio design.
15. EVOLUTIONARY SEARCH (the deepest algorithm — Hayek's spontaneous order, our loop):
    variation → selection → memory → reproduction. Not an analogy; it IS the protocol.
