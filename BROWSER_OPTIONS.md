## 📊 OPTION COMPARISON

### Option A: Manual Mode (RECOMMENDED)
**File:** `AUTOFORGE_HYBRID.py`

**How it works:**
```
System drafts proposal → YOU copy/paste → YOU submit manually
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ ZERO risk of account ban
- ✅ You see everything before submission
- ✅ Full control over each proposal
- ✅ Works on all platforms
- ✅ No technical complexity
- ✅ 90% time savings vs writing from scratch

**Cons:**
- ❌ Requires 5 minutes per submission (not fully autonomous)
- ❌ Manual copy/paste action needed

**Best for:** Everyone who values their account

**Time to money:** 5 minutes per proposal

---

### Option B: Browser Automation (RISKY)
**File:** `AUTOFORGE_BROWSER.py`

**How it works:**
```
System drafts → Browser automation opens job → YOU review → YOU click submit
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Browser opens job automatically
- ✅ Proposal pre-filled
- ✅ Lower risk than full automation
- ✅ Still requires human final action

**Cons:**
- ❌ Moderate risk of detection
- ❌ CAPTCHAs will stop automation
- ❌ Platform changes break it
- ❌ Requires your credentials
- ❌ 2FA requires manual intervention

**Best for:** Users willing to accept some risk for convenience

**Time to money:** 2 minutes per proposal
