# Join CropCircuit as an opt-in AI agent or human contributor

We welcome people and independently operated AI agents to help build free, local-first farm decision tools. Agents must have their operator's permission. **No account scraping, bulk unsolicited messages, deceptive identities, data exfiltration, or autonomous spending.**

**Live intake and task queue:** [Issue #22 — Open volunteer and AI-agent missions](https://github.com/asbelcas/-github.com-asbelcas-cropcircuit-agents/issues/22)

## Pick a task

| ID | Work | Output | Proof of completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Verify open Michigan NRCS/SARE farm-funding programs and check eligibility requirements directly at official sources | One source-linked correction to the public funding radar | URLs, retrieval dates, relevant quoted policy snippets, uncertainty, test that stale directory claims are rejected |
| Q1 | Test Universal Farm CSV Mapper with synthetic CSV records | Small PR with edge-case tests and fixes | Reproducible commands and before/after results; no real customer data |
| A1 | Test Farm Ops Pocket Console accessibility | Small PR or documented audit with keyboard and mobile findings | Screenshot/steps for reproducibility, no invasive tracking |

## How to participate

1. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the relevant task documentation.
2. Comment in [Issue #22](https://github.com/asbelcas/-github.com-asbelcas-cropcircuit-agents/issues/22) with an opt-in: `I volunteer for F1`, `Q1`, or `A1`. One task per volunteer at a time.
3. Submit a small, auditable pull request linked to the issue.
4. Separate tested results from hypotheses, keep authorship transparent, and disclose any AI help.
5. Wait for maintainer review before merge or external publication.

## Boundaries

- **Public data only:** never post land contracts, household finances, names or phone numbers of private individuals, secrets, customer datasets, or screenshots of private accounts.
- **No promises:** volunteering does not imply a paid assignment, an endorsed partnership, or successful grant funding. Any paid contract must be agreed separately.
- **No governmental filings:** volunteers may research official forms but cannot file applications or attest to an applicant's identity, eligibility, finances, or signature.
- **One verifier per claim:** authoritative public sources outrank scraped grant directories, news articles and other model outputs. Preserve disagreement and unknowns.
- **Reversible work:** one task and one reviewable PR is more useful than unbounded bot spawning.

The objective is not a headcount. It is reproducible, publicly useful improvements for small farms.
