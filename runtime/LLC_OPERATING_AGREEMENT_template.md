1.3 Purpose: farm systems consulting, agricultural advisory services, and
    sale of digital products and related services, and any lawful business.

Article II — Member
2.1 Sole member: Casey Armstrong ("Member"), 100% ownership.
2.2 Capital contribution: initial contribution of $[amount] and/or services;
    no additional contributions required unless Member decides.

Article III — Management
3.1 Member-managed: all authority vests in the Member.
3.2 Delegation: the Member may delegate operational tasks to agents,
    contractors, employees, and software systems ("Operators"). Delegation
    does not transfer ownership, fiduciary duty, or legal accountability,
    which remain with the Member.
3.3 Operator governance policy: the Company adopts as policy the typed
    resource calculus at runtime/FLORAL_CIPHER_v2.md, including the
    certificate rule (Claim[X] ≰ Cert[X]; no resource from narration alone)
    and the no-signalling rule of commerce. Operators may propose states;
    only authorized instruments may certify them. This policy governs all
    automated writes to Company shared state.

Article IV — Distributions
4.1 Distributions to the Member at times and amounts determined by the
    Member, subject to Michigan law and Company obligations.

Article V — Books, Records, Money
5.1 Separate Company bank account; Company funds never commingle with
    personal funds.
5.2 Books kept on a calendar-year basis; records available to the Member.
5.3 Tax treatment: disregarded entity (default) unless the Member elects
    otherwise with professional advice.

Article VI — Liability and Indemnification
6.1 No Member liability beyond capital contribution, per the Act.
6.2 Company indemnifies Member for actions taken in good faith on behalf of
    the Company.

Article VII — Dissolution
7.1 Dissolution upon Member decision, sale of substantially all assets, or
    as required by law.
