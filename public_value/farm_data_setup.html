What is included

1. Source-file cleanupUp to 3 CSV/XLSX exports, up to 1,500 total data rows, for one farm operation.
2. Field mappingLabor, recurring chores, equipment maintenance, parts inventory, harvest, and/or direct-market sales are mapped only where the source data supports it.
3. Portable deliverablesCanonical CSV files, a Workspace 1.2 JSON backup, and a mapping/quality note describing what was changed or left unresolved.
4. Operations snapshotA coarse shareable summary is generated when the supplied records support it. Sales dollars remain excluded unless requested.
5. One correction roundOne reasonable correction pass is included if a field was mapped incorrectly or a source convention needs clarification.
6. No lock-inThe output is ordinary CSV/JSON and can be inspected, stored, or transformed outside CropCircuit.

Good fit

  • Your farm records exist, but they are scattered across spreadsheets or exports.
  • Column names changed over time or different sheets use different conventions.
  • You want a clean portable starting point without learning a new farm-management platform first.
  • You want to use CropCircuit's local tools but do not want to manually re-enter old records.

Not included

  • bookkeeping, tax classification, or accounting cleanup;
  • bank, credit-card, payroll, medical, employee HR, or customer-payment-card data;
  • inventing missing farm measurements or guessing ambiguous values;
  • mechanical compatibility claims for alternate equipment parts;
  • ongoing cloud hosting or a managed database;
  • custom software development beyond the fixed mapping/setup scope.

If a source field is ambiguous, it is flagged rather than silently guessed.

What to prepare

Prepare up to three operational exports. Remove credentials, Social Security numbers, payment-card details, medical information, and customer personal information before sending anything.

Useful source files include labor/time logs, chore lists, maintenance sheets, parts lists, harvest records, and farm-market/CSA sales exports.
