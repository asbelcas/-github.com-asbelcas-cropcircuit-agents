Weekly Farm Operations Brief

A private operating picture built from this browser's CropCircuit workspace: labor, harvest, direct-market sales, equipment maintenance, and upcoming recurring chores.

Descriptive, not a scorecard: the brief does not infer profit from sales, revenue from harvest, or inefficiency from time spent. Free-text notes are deliberately omitted.
