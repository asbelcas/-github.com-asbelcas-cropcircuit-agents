Synthetic Demo Workspace

Load a small fictional farm dataset into this browser so you can try CropCircuit's local-first tools before entering any real records.

Every demo record is synthetic. Demo IDs begin with demo-. You can remove them again without deleting normal records.

What to try next

After loading the demo, open the Weekly Farm Operations Brief, Labor Opportunity Analyzer, Schedule Estimate Calibrator, Parts Inventory, Harvest Log, or Sales Log from the same web origin. They will read the compatible local records.

Browser storage is origin-scoped. A demo loaded under a temporary preview origin will not automatically appear under a different future Pages or cropcircuit.org origin.
