Farm Labor Opportunity Analyzer

Import a time-study CSV, annualize the observed work, and see which repeated tasks consume the most labor dollars. The tool ranks opportunity; it does not tell you what equipment to buy.

Private analysis: your CSV is read in the browser only. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted.
