Farm Decision Calculator

Five transparent calculations for common farm decisions. Nothing is sent anywhere: this is a single offline HTML file with no analytics, accounts, or external dependencies.

Use your own assumptions. The calculator intentionally does not embed species consumption rates, contractor prices, wage rates, or biological defaults. Those vary by farm, weather, system, and region.
Arithmetic is downstream of values. When a decision has hard ecological, animal-welfare, human, legal, safety, or resilience boundaries, use the Right Relationship Decision Gate first. A good ROI does not automatically make an option acceptable.

Enter your numbers and calculate.

2. Simple payback

3. Fence-line estimate

Starts from the perimeter of a square parcel, then lets you increase it for irregular shape and add internal fence.

Useful for early budgeting, not surveying.

4. Water storage

Supply your own appropriate consumption rate.
